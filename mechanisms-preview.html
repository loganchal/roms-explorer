How the budget is constructed

Each physical process changes temperature and salinity. The model converts these tracer tendencies to density tendencies, then calculates each process’s contribution to the change in potential energy anomaly (PEA).

ρ̇j = ρ0(−αṪj + βṠj)

φ̇j = (g/H) ∫−H0 (ρ̄̇j − ρ̇j) z dz

Integrating these contributions through time, and adding the initial PEA and closure corrections, reconstructs the developing stratification. The layer extends from the surface to 200 m. Corrections account for nonlinear density, moving geometry and tracer-update residuals.

This example uses one preselected offshore column, September 2008–February 2009. The budget uses online model diagnostics. The profile snapshots are daily means; surface-referenced potential density is calculated from their T and S and shown as σθ = ρθ − 1000 kg m⁻³. Accumulated contributions are relative to the start of the season, not a climatological anomaly.
